The roadmap

Most roadmaps are promises. This one is mostly receipts. Three phases are complete, the fourth is live, and every green check below links to the transaction or the document that makes it true.

1

LUV is priceless complete

The gesture is born: a fixed 111-quadrillion supply, fee-free wallet to wallet, and a free trillion LUV to everyone who arrived. Abundance over scarcity.

2

The value of LUV complete

The LUV/ETH market opened, seeded at the absolute maximum a Uniswap V2 pair can hold. The gesture met the market, and the fee engine was proven live.

3

The price of LUV complete

The pair creates it, Uniswap expresses it, the oracle measures it every minute. The liquidity behind it is locked, and the site speaks the price in every denomination.

4

Share the LUV live

Sharing is the marketing. The LUVdrip meter runs a million LUV a day per participant, the earn rail is built, and the LUV drop is honoured on Ethereum, 1 LUV == 1 LUV.

5

LUV immersive next

LUV as the social currency of presence inside DeltaVerse spatial worlds, where a gesture is something you make, not just click.
