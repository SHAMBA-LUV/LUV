II. 1 LUV is always == 1 LUV

1 LUV === 1 LUV

Here is the thing most tokens can't say. One LUV is exactly one LUV — always. Send a friend 1 LUV and 1 LUV arrives: no fee, no minimum, no rounding, no "dust". LUV carries eighteen decimals of full precision and the contract never approximates — a gesture of 0.000000000000000001 LUV is a real gesture, delivered whole. Wallet-to-wallet, LUV is identical to itself, every time.

The price of LUV moves — it is created by the LUV / ETH pair, expressed on Uniswap, measured by the oracle, and today it is 10.8× where it was born. But the price is a description of LUV; it is not LUV. Your million LUV is a million LUV on the day the chart is red and a million LUV on the day it is green. That is what makes it a unit you can gesture with: the meaning of the gesture never changes size.

IV. Rule one — keep your private key private

Never share it. Anyone with your private key controls your wallet and every LUV in it — today's LUV and every LUV that ever arrives. There is no reset, no support ticket, no "forgot my key". The chain does not know who you are; it only knows who can sign.
  • No one from SHAMBA LUV will ever ask for it. Not by email, not on Telegram, not in a DM, not "to verify your airdrop". Anyone who asks is stealing.
  • Never paste it into a website. Not ours, not a "checker", not a "claim portal". The only place your key belongs is inside a wallet you control.
  • Never photograph it to a cloud. A screenshot synced to three services is a key published three times.
  • Write it down, offline. Paper or metal, two copies, two places — the way people have kept keys since 2009. Paper does not get phished.
  • Your signature is your identity. That is how BANKON verifies who you are and how LUV knows a gesture came from you. Sovereignty over custody; consent over default. Your folder belongs to you.

Lose the key and the LUV is gone the way the 4.98 quadrillion at 0x…dEaD is gone — provably, forever, to nobody. Please don't send yours there by accident.

V. Rule two (the better one) — put your key in a trust, for release at a later time

Keeping a key private protects LUV from other people. Putting the key beyond your own reach for a stated term protects LUV from the one person a private key cannot guard against — you, on a bad day.

A trust is any arrangement in which the key is held so that it cannot be used until a date you chose in advance. As formal or as simple as you like:

formhow it works
a sealed envelopeKey on paper, sealed, dated, given to someone you trust with written instructions: do not open before ___. The oldest trust there is.
safe-deposit / lawyer's escrowThe same envelope, held by an institution or an attorney under a release instruction. Formal, dated, witnessed.
a legal trust or willThe key (or its location) in an estate instrument with a release condition — a date, an age, an event. How LUV outlives you and reaches the people you meant it for.
split custodyThe key split into shares (Shamir's secret sharing) held by several trustees with the same release date. No single person — including you — can reconstruct it early.
the LUVlocker (on-chain)The extend-only vault: lock the principal for a term, keep earning reflections the whole time. Use it when the FAQ links a live lock — earmarked is not locked, and a lock is a transaction anyone can verify.
locker as a service (coming)The trust above, done for you on-chain from your dashboard — choose the term, lock the principal, keep the reflections — once proof of redemption and collect are finalized. Redeem and collect are the rails the lock will ride; they ship first. Until the FAQ links it live, it is a promise, not a product.

Choose the date honestly. A year is a real commitment. Five years is a statement. The point is not the instrument — it is that the release date is chosen now, calmly, and cannot be moved by the person you will be on the day the price does something.

VI. What a locked key does — three things

1 · it protects LUV

A key that cannot be used cannot be phished, drained, panic-sold, or talked out of you. Every attack on a wallet is an attack on access; a trust removes access for the term. Your LUV sits at your address on the verified contract — visible to anyone, touchable by no one.

2 · it shows your commitment to LUV

Holding is visible. Your balance is a public line in the holders table; a balance that does not move through every dip is the on-chain shape of trust. A key in trust is the trust game's first move — LUV extended forward in time before the return is known — and it says, in a way no tweet can: I am here for the term, not the tick. Community actions are the measure of community sentiment; the most legible action of all is the one you don't take.

3 · it creates permanent value

Every LUV not sold is depth for everyone — LUV that cannot move cannot be dumped, and each exit is cushioned for those who remain. The 3% pays you to hold — a locked balance earns reflections the whole term with no action required. Metcalfe compounds it — you stay a node while n grows, and the bridges you formed keep counting. A term ends; the value doesn't — at release you hold LUV in a network larger, deeper and older than the one you entered, having proven on the ledger that you were there from the start.
