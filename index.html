Holding pays you3% of every trade reflects to every holder, automatically

Each buy or sell through the pair carries a 5% fee. Three fifths of it is distributed pro-rata to every wallet holding LUV, in the same transaction. Your balance rises without you signing anything.

how it is engineered

Reflection is a rebasing ledger: the contract tracks a reflected supply and a true supply, and each holder's balance is their share of the reflected total. The fee shrinks the reflected supply, so everyone's share grows. The pair and the burn address are excluded, so reflections go to people, not to the pool or the void.

The liquidity is locked100% of the LUV/WETH position, in a contract with no owner

The treasury's entire LP holding sits in an extend-only timelock. No pause, no rescue, no admin. Not even the team that deployed it can bring the maturity forward. You can read the lock from Ethereum with your own browser.

how it is engineered

Two gates, time and block height, must both pass. A lock can be made longer by its beneficiary, never shorter by anyone. The locker was deployed through the deterministic Create3d factory, so its six-leading-1s address is reproducible on every EVM chain, and only by the treasury.

The pool was seeded at the Uniswap V2 maximum, 2¹¹²−1 wei of LUV. The price is quoted as USD per trillion LUV because a trillion is the natural unit of a 111-quadrillion supply. ETH/USD is the median of two on-chain stablecoin pairs and one external mirror.
