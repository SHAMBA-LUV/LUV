what makes this a lock, and not a promise

No owner, anywhere. No pause, no rescue, no setter, no proxy, no delegatecall, no SELFDESTRUCT, no tx.origin. Nobody — including the deployer — can bring a maturity forward. Extension is one-way: a lock can be made longer by its beneficiary, never shorter by anyone.

Two gates. A lock is bound by time, by block height, or by both, and opens only when the later has passed — a clock cannot open it early, a stalling chain cannot strand it.

Deterministic identity (cypherpunk4096): deployed via CREATE3, the locker's address is a function of (deployer, salt) alone — reproducible on every EVM chain, claimable only by the SHAMBA LUV treasury. The six leading 1s are mined, not luck — and not a different trust story: the contract has no owner regardless of who deployed it.

186 tests, including a mainnet-fork rehearsal that locked the real LUV/WETH position and proved it comes back to the wei — and one open item stated plainly: the code is self-audited, not yet independently audited.
