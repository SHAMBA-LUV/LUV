LUV was priceless before it had a price

chapter two · the arithmetic #supply

A supply made only of ones

The total supply is a repunit — eighteen ones, a point, eighteen ones — fixed at genesis and never mintable again:

111,111,111,111,111,111.111111111111111111 LUV

And 4,980,559,643,425,539 LUV — 4.4825% of supply — is burned to the dEaD address: reflection-excluded, out of circulation forever. Down to the last wei, the numbers are exact; the engine treats rounding as a display decision and never a storage one.

chapter three · phase 2 #market

The pair creates the price

LUV trades against WETH in a Uniswap V2 pool, and the price is read from the pool's own reserves, block-anchored — never from an aggregator's opinion of it. The pool is also the fee engine: 5% on buys and sells against it — 3% reflections · 1% liquidity · 1% team, the team point governed by the DAIO in three equal parts with 2-of-3 consensus. The 3% is why holding is the position: every trade pays every holder, automatically.

chapter four · the LUVdrip #drip

A million LUV a day, earned by logging in — metered to the last atom

Logging in starts the LUVdrip: 1,000,000 LUV a day — 11.574 LUV every second — and it keeps dripping for the whole 24 hours that sign-in armed, tab open or closed, until the full million has landed; the next million starts with the next log in. It does not mint per second, because 1,000,000 LUV a day does not divide evenly into 86,400 seconds and rounding is forbidden. So the drip separates counting from settling: DRIP accrues exactly into one growing tally, and REDEEM delivers that whole tally on chain as one integer — the participant paying the gas from their own wallet, or the project sponsoring it for them. Nothing is rounded — only deferred.

chapter five · phase 3 · the engine #pulse

One heartbeat, everywhere, owned by no one

LUV built its own engine from the field rather than adopting one. Its first organ is a clock that is also a heart: 60 beats per minute, exactly 1 Hz, its phase taken from corrected wall-clock time — so every heart on every page, for every visitor on earth, beats in the same phase. Nothing sounds and nothing is stored without an explicit tap. The beat never phones home.

chapter six · the origin #polygon

Where LUV began, LUV returns

Polygon was the first deployment. Every holder on the Polygon contract will be honoured; that commitment stands, and the snapshot is being reconstructed.
